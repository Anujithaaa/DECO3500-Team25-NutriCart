Nutri Cart 🥗📱
Making healthier grocery choices easier for university students

DECO3500 — Social & Mobile Computing | Team 25

🌱 Project Overview

How can we enhance healthy food choices for younger university students during in-person grocery shopping through social and contextual computing?

ConnectX explores how technology can support university students in making healthier, more informed, and more practical food choices while physically shopping for groceries.

Rather than expecting students to completely change their shopping habits, our project investigates how social interactions, contextual information, and mobile technology can be integrated into the grocery-shopping experience at the moment decisions are made.

🎯 The Challenge

University life can make healthy grocery shopping difficult.

Students often have to balance:

💰 Limited budgets
⏱️ Busy schedules
🛒 Quick purchasing decisions
🥗 Limited nutritional knowledge
👥 Social influences
📱 Information overload

This creates an opportunity to design technology that provides meaningful support at the right moment — while students are actually shopping.

💡 Our Approach

ConnectX investigates the possibilities of combining:

📱 Mobile Computing
Providing accessible, real-time support through a student's mobile device.

🌐 Contextual Computing
Understanding the user's shopping context and providing relevant information when it matters.

👥 Social Computing
Exploring how friends, peers, shared goals and social interactions can influence healthier decisions.

🥦 Healthy Decision-Making
Helping students compare and understand food choices without making grocery shopping complicated.

🚀 Project Vision

Make the healthier choice the easier choice.

ConnectX aims to create a grocery-shopping experience where technology supports students without disrupting the way they naturally shop.

The goal isn't to tell students what they should buy — it's to give them the right information, context and social support to make better decisions for themselves.

👥 Target Users

Primary users:
🎓 Younger university students who regularly purchase groceries in person.

Our design will consider students who may:

Shop on a limited budget
Have limited time for meal planning
Live independently for the first time
Make spontaneous grocery decisions
Be influenced by friends and peers
Want to eat healthier but struggle with consistency
