## Round 1

**Home Hub (G01-P1)**
- Confused about navigation: how the barcode scanner is meant to work at home; recipe cards don't show cost, time or servings.
- Unsure whether prices were for every store or only Woolworths.
- Didn't like Housemate requests: "message me what you want instead... feels like another group chat".
- Didn't need Already at home but said it depends on the person; "they already have an idea of what they need".
- Shared list is good if buying for a group.
- Overall it felt like "multi step authentication". "With the tech now, it feels very questionable and redundant to use... too much effort when I can just use my phone or another grocery app that does it easier/simpler."
- Called the Hub idea "counterproductive and counter intuitive": "instead of shopping together, just sends list or asks online sort of like a group chat feature".
- Felt it is "promoting the opposite of being social"; thinks it suits introverts.
- Named Coles Click and Collect as simpler and faster.

**Cart Unit (G01-P2)**
- "Feels like another grocery app."
- Liked the idea of Compare.
- Confused by Split Shop: "How does the other person get the split list if there's only one device? Does the device split in half?"
- Housemate requests and Already at home: "Sometimes I forget about what's in the house so this'll help me sometimes, but this can be also done through just messaging me."
- "It would be nice if this was its own thing, it would be cool if it somehow picks up messages between our group chat in SMS."
- Loved the remaining budget: "I always think twice on if I have enough budget to buy all of these."

## Round 2 (swapped)

**Home Hub (G01-P2)**
- Also confused by the navigation.
- Understood requests and Already at home, but: "I can just use a phone and either call or message them about it... the only time I can see myself using this is when I'm talking to someone in the grocery store."
- "Too many steps for a shopping list. I get it's shared but a simple 'one whole chicken, one milk' etc in a notepad or on the group chat is so much faster and better."
- Liked the meal ideas: "We're pretty indecisive sometimes when picking food so seeing what we can make with the ingredients at home is pretty sweet."
- Already at home: "I can see ourselves not doing this after a while, this seems super tedious."

**Cart Unit (G01-P1)**
- "Similar to the home hub, this feels like extra steps."
- Understood Housemate requests from this side but kept the view from round 1.
- "Deal alert is pretty nice to have but I wish it shows location."
- Split Shop: understood it, but "Shouldn't the social aspect be going together rather than away from each other?"
- Overall: "This has clear features that make sense, but I don't see a use and the social aspects of this needs improving."

## LED notifications
- Both said they are not needed.
- G01-P1: "If I wanted to let them know about something then I call them, or I message in the group chat if it isn't so much important."
- G01-P2: "I would rather a sound or a chime to let me and other people know what needs attention or a reply... would probably not even notice the lights."

## Overall
- Neither would use it in its current form.
- G01-P1: "This feels like another notepad and a roundabout way of a grocery app. I won't use this if its only use was for reminding and grocery shopping... This doesn't really feel social at all, feels like a glorified grocery app."
- G01-P2: "I understand where this product comes from, but I don't see the use of this. Feels very old or redundant with the technology of today... I can just use our group chat that we have, paste it into the notes app and have a similar use to the devices. Feels and looks clunky as well, both the home hub and cart unit."
