## G02-P1
- Saw the point of it, but found it not engaging enough.
- The interface isn't very friendly, and navigation needs work for first-time users.
- Best part: product comparison and seeing the price in one place.
- Suggested adding a chat box and a calling feature.
- Would give it a try because it is new and people aren't familiar with something like it.

## G02-P2
- The communication part is good: housemates can post recipes, suggestions and messages for the shop.
- You don't have to guess or buy anything twice, because you can see what is already at home.
- Comparison, prices and offers are good. Split Shop is handy.
- Downside: a bit dull, and hard for a first-time user to understand and navigate.

## Both
- Worth trying if it saves significant money and significant time, because students struggle with budgeting.
