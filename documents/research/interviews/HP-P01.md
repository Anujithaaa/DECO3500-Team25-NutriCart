## Notes

*[interviewer] - First person (interviewed and shown old prototype before)*

- Likes the current prototype than the previous one.
  - Liked the handheld cart unit but they use a shopping basket sometimes and this will be a pain to carry around
  - They said it was a bit too big for them, if it was a bit smaller to carry it around easier
- The three screens (shopping list, housemate suggestions/alerts and comparison) are good, “I can see myself use the comparison and alerts page a lot”
  - For the shopping list, they really liked the budget, in cart and remaining budget display. “Sometimes I forget my budget so seeing what’s remaining will really help”
    - Wants to see specific brands for some ingredients (e.g. “instead of 1 pack of chicken which specific brand are we getting it from”
    - Wants to see unit prices/option to see unit price
    - Thought seeing who added what was alright, doesn’t see a point in it unless they have allergies/sorting who bought what to know how much that person will pay them for groceries
  - Doesn’t like the comparison page as much, “I grab whatever's familiar or cheapest and move on. I can see it being useful for bigger purchases, like when I'm actually unsure, but if it adds any real delay to a normal trip I'll skip it entirely.”
    - Wants an option to add more products
  - They liked the alerts mainly for the deals. They liked the housemate suggestions “I can see myself going to the grocery store earlier than I usually do to avoid traffic of people while my housemate adds to the shopping list and suggestions”
- “Yeah I think I can see myself using this but adding the ingredients and suggestions to the home unit will be a pain to do for me and my housemates”
