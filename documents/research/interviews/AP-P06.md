## Notes

### Q: Do you shop with other people usually? If so who? If not why?

**Participant:** Usually I shop by myself because it's easier and faster. Sometimes I go with my friends, especially if we're already going out together. When I shop alone, I can just get what I need without spending too much time.

### Follow-up: Tell me about the last time you shopped with someone else.

**Participant:** Last weekend I went shopping with one of my friends. We were at IGA and she wanted to make a pasta dish, so we ended up buying the ingredients together. I actually picked up a different pasta sauce because she said the one she normally buys tasted better. So I went with her recommendation.

**Budget, Time & Convenience**

### Q: How does budget, time, and convenience influence what you buy?

**Participant:** Budget is definitely important because groceries can become expensive. I usually have an idea of how much I want to spend before I go. Time also matters. If I'm in a rush, I'll buy something that's easy to prepare rather than ingredients that need a lot of cooking.

### Follow-up: Tell me about the last time you were in a rush or tight on money.

**Participant:** I had classes that day and didn't have much time, so instead of buying ingredients for a proper meal I bought bread, eggs and some fruit. It was cheaper and I could make something quickly.

**Choosing Products**

### Q: How do you decide what products to choose when grocery shopping?

**Participant:** I usually look at the price first, then the ingredients and sometimes the nutrition information. If I've bought the product before and liked it, I normally just buy the same one.

### Follow-up: Think of the last time you compared two similar products.

**Participant:** I was comparing two types of yoghurt. One was cheaper but had more sugar. The other one was slightly more expensive but seemed healthier, so I chose that one.

**What Does "Healthy" Mean?**

### Q: Tell me about the last time you thought, "this is the healthy option."

**Participant:** Probably when I bought yoghurt and fruit. I consider things with less added sugar and more natural ingredients healthier. I compared the yoghurt with another flavoured yoghurt that had more sugar.

### Q: Has anyone ever told you something was healthy that surprised you or that you disagreed with?

**Participant:** Yes, sometimes my friends send things on Instagram or TikTok saying something is healthy, but when I check the ingredients it doesn't always seem that healthy. I don't automatically believe it. I usually check the nutrition label myself.

**Living Situation**

### Q: How does your living situation affect your grocery shopping?

**Participant:** It affects it quite a lot because I have to think about what I can actually use before it goes bad. If I'm shopping just for myself, I don't want to buy a huge amount of fresh food.

### Follow-up: Who did you buy for recently?

**Participant:** Mostly myself. Sometimes I buy things that my housemates can also use, like milk, bread or snacks. If they already have something at home, I won't buy another one.

**Shopping for Others**

### Q: If you shop for others, what special considerations are there?

**Participant:** You have to think about what they like and what they don't eat. For example, if I'm cooking with my housemates, I'll buy ingredients that everyone is okay with instead of just buying what I personally like.

**Paying More for Products**

### Q: Is there a product you pay more for? Why?

**Participant:** Yes, sometimes I pay more for things like yoghurt or coffee if I know the quality is better. I definitely look at the cheaper option first, but if the difference isn't huge, I'll usually choose the one I prefer.

**Bulk vs Small Amounts**

### Q: Do you buy in bulk or small amounts?

**Participant:** Mostly small amounts because I'm shopping for myself. Things like rice or pasta I might buy in bigger quantities because they don't expire quickly. But for vegetables and fruit, I prefer smaller amounts.

**Avoiding Products**

### Q: Is there anything you avoid buying or buy less of?

**Participant:** I try not to buy too many sugary snacks or soft drinks. Sometimes I still buy them, but I try to limit them.

### Follow-up: Tell me about something you put back recently.

**Participant:** I picked up a chocolate bar but put it back because I realised I already had snacks at home. It wasn't really necessary.

**Fresh, Canned or Frozen**

### Q: How do you decide between fresh, canned or frozen?

**Participant:** It depends on what it is. For vegetables I sometimes buy frozen because they last longer and are convenient. For things like fruit, I prefer fresh if I know I'll eat it soon.

**Changes in Shopping**

### Q: What's something you changed about how you shop in the last year?

**Participant:** I've become more conscious about checking prices and nutrition labels. Before, I would just pick the brand I recognised. Now I compare things more.

**Prototype Testing**

**Interviewer:** This is a prototype for a grocery shopping app. Take a minute to look around and tell me what you're thinking as you go, like you're talking out loud.

**Participant:** Okay. I can see that it's connected to grocery shopping and there's something about friends and healthier meals.

### Q: What do you think this app is for, before I explain anything?

**Participant:** I think it's an app that helps you decide what to buy when you're grocery shopping, but also gives you recommendations from your friends.

### Q: Walk me through what you're seeing on the home screen.

**Participant:** I can see the store I'm shopping at, then friend recommendations and a group goal. I think the idea is that the app knows where I'm shopping and gives me things that are relevant to that store. The group goal is interesting because it makes healthy eating feel more social.

*The prototype's home screen identifies the store, provides friend recommendations and presents a shared goal such as cooking three healthier meals.*

**Friend Recommendations**

### Q: What do you think about the friend recommendations feature?

**Participant:** I actually like this because I trust recommendations from my friends more than random recommendations. If I see that my friend has made something and liked it, I might want to try it too.

### Q: Would seeing what your friends recommend influence what you buy?

**Participant:** Yes, especially if it's something easy and cheap. If someone says, "This is easy to make before uni," that would make me more likely to save it.

### Q: What do you think about being able to save a friend's recommendation?

**Participant:** That's useful because I could save it and then look at it when I'm actually shopping.

*The prototype allows users to save friends' recommended meals and view the associated recipe.*

**Group Goal**

### Q: What do you think about the group goal?

**Participant:** I think it's motivating. If I know my friends are also trying to cook healthier meals, I might actually do it rather than just saying I'm going to eat healthier.

### Q: Would seeing your friends' progress motivate you?

**Participant:** A little bit. I think it would make it more fun, but I wouldn't want it to feel competitive or like I'm being judged.
