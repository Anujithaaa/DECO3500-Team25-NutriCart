# Interview records (de-identified)

One record per session. Each record has a participant code, the study, the interviewer, consent, links to other sessions with the same person, and the transcript or notes with identifying details removed.

**Codes:** `R1` Round 1 exploratory interviews · `AP` app probe (v1) prototype sessions · `HP` NutriCart Hub physical prototype sessions · `SH` in-store shadowing ([SH-P01](../shadowing/SH-P01.md)) · `ST` studio peer sessions. Codes are per **session**; where the same person took part again, the record says so under *Linked records*.

**How records were de-identified:** speaker labels standardised to Interviewer and Participant; names of participants and the people they mention, exact ages, course and year details, home suburbs and similar details replaced with descriptions in [square brackets]; timestamps removed; wording otherwise kept as transcribed. Each record lists the *types* of redaction made. Original recordings and full transcripts are held privately by the team and are not shared outside it, as participants were told.
