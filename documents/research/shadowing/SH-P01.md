### When and why they use their phone while shopping

Didn't use their phone to influence thier shopping just for the shopping list.

### Whether they use a shopping list and how closely they follow it

Shopping list used but only for item they don't want to forget, other than that they buy what they feel like.

### How price affects their choices

Only bought things that were on sale even for the essentials only bought the cheapest option due to budget

### How they choose between healthier and less healthy options

Chose "healthy" options when it came to fresh produce but also tended to go for less "healthy" options when it came to convenience and snacks.
- Zucchini, fresh corn, avocado.
- Bakery items, high sugar juice, biscuits.

### When they change their mind about a product and why

They wanted to get a snack from the "health" section but realised that there were still lots of calories is the specific item and didn't get it

### Whether they seek advice or recommendations from friends

They don't seek out any recommendations and says they usually only make foods they know or are comfortable making. One exception to buying items not on special is when they really want to make a dish and need a specific ingredient-> they are very adamant on following a recipe exactly

### Whether social media influences what they buy

Lots of the time yes, but today it didn't influence any choices

### Whether they shop based on a specific meal or recipe

Today they were shopping based on buy food to meal prep for the coming weeks since they'll be busy with lots of uni work

### Unplanned purchases and what triggered them

Marked down items, specials (like 1/2 price) and also one items they had previously and just remembered how good it was so they got it again (not in special)

### Whether being short on time changes their choices

As a hypothetical they said that if they were in a rush for items they knew it wouldn't change anything but for items they were unfamiliar with they would choose the cheapest option and not really have time to compare anything else

### What information they check before buying: price, nutrition, ingredients, brand, etc.

1. price
2. nutrition
3. ingredients
4. brand

### Moments where they seem unsure or struggle to make a decision

Price, do they actually needed it

### Moments where a friend recommendation, recipe, or app suggestion could have been useful

Usual like to buy some meals based on social media posts they've seen, but today they were only buying essentials and other cheaper items if desired, not committing to any recipe

### Moments where an app notification or recommendation would likely be ignored or annoying

User didn't really use their phone and even if they said that if they got the notification it would not really sway them unless it was something they didn't see at Woolies since they also like looking at the mark down items.
