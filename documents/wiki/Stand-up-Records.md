# Stand-up Records

Internal team stand-ups and meetings. Each entry records **state**, **outcomes** and **next steps**.

> **How this page was made.** We did not post records weekly during Weeks 3–10. On 13 October 2026 we backfilled them from studio notes, our OneDrive files and team chat. Backfilled entries are marked *(backfilled)*. Entries from Week 11 onwards are written on the day.

**How we coordinated.** Until 14 October we coordinated through our WhatsApp group, a shared OneDrive folder, the plan of work in our Team Charter and check-ins at studio. From 14 October we tracked tasks on the [GitHub Projects board](https://github.com/users/MaxCoder07/projects/1), where each of us moves our own cards and closes our own issues. WhatsApp is still used for quick messages.

Usual meeting: Mondays 9:30am, plus straight after studio when needed.
