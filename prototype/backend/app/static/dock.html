What to note in every session

  • Who adds items and requests, and who never touches the Dock (Grudin: who does the work, who benefits).
  • Does anyone text a housemate instead of using Ask home? Why?
  • What the shopper looks at first on the Cart Unit, what they ignore, anything that slows them down.
  • Reactions to the cost split: fair, awkward, useful?
  • First-use comprehension: "What do you think this is for?" before any explanation.
